# Generative AI & Large Language Model (LLM) Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-LLM-01  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026  
**Applicability:** All employees, contractors, and third-party partners.

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the acceptable, safe, and compliant use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Large Language Models (LLMs) within the organization. The goal is to drive innovation while mitigating risks related to data leakage, intellectual property infringement, and algorithmic hallucinations.

## 2. Permitted Tools
Employees must strictly categorize tools into approved and prohibited classes before inputting any company data:

* **Approved Enterprise Tools:** Only tools wrapped in enterprise-grade data privacy agreements (e.g., dedicated corporate Copilot instances, private enterprise API endpoints) may be used with internal business data.
* **Prohibited Public Tools:** Free, public-tier consumer models (e.g., standard free-tier web chatbots) are strictly prohibited for work-related tasks involving non-public data, as their prompts may be used for model retraining.

## 3. Data Classification & Input Restrictions
Data entering any AI system must comply with corporate data governance standards:

* **🔴 Public/Consumer Tools:** Only public marketing copy, generic programming syntax, or unclassified informational text may be submitted.
* **🟡 Internal Private Tools:** Proprietary codebases, strategic drafts, and operational plans may be submitted *only* if the system explicitly guarantees data exemption from model retraining.
* **🚫 Strictly Prohibited Material:** Under no circumstances should **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**, protected health data, customer credentials, financial records, or active corporate trade secrets be entered into any LLM prompt.

## 4. Output Verification & "Human-in-the-Loop"
AI-generated outputs cannot be trusted blindly:

* **Fact-Checking Requirement:** Employees are fully responsible for validating the factual accuracy of AI-generated content. All text, calculations, and references must be verified against source material.
* **Code Review Safeguards:** AI-generated source code must undergo standard automated security vulnerability scanning and peer review before introduction into any production branch.
* **Bias and Inclusion Alert:** Users must review outputs for subtle demographic bias, toxic generalizations, or exclusionary language before distributing external-facing material.

## 5. Intellectual Property & Attribution
* **Copyright Boundaries:** Do not generate or distribute AI outputs that explicitly copy or closely mimic copyrighted brand names, visual logos, or patented text blocks.
* **Attribution Standard:** When AI tools generate more than 30% of a critical business report, legal document, or customer-facing manual, a clear transparency disclosure must be appended (e.g., *"This document was drafted with the assistance of automated language systems"*).

---

## 6. Policy Enforcement
Any violation of this policy may result in the immediate revocation of system privileges, internal disciplinary action, or termination of employment contracts.
