# Corporate Ethical AI Charter

**Policy ID:** POL-ETH-01  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 10, 2026  
**Applicability:** All executive leadership, research teams, software engineers, and product managers.

---

## 1. Vision Statement
As an organization, we recognize that artificial intelligence is a powerful tool capable of transforming operations and driving massive innovation. However, unchecked algorithmic deployments introduce risks to human rights, privacy, and social equity. This charter establishes the binding ethical baseline that guides our research, procurement, development, and deployment of automated systems.

---

## 2. Core Ethical Principles

### 2.1 Human Autonomy & Oversight
* **Human-Centric Design:** AI systems must be designed to augment, empower, and support human capabilities, not to replace human agency or dignity.
* **The Right to Review:** For any system executing automated decisions that affect employment, creditworthiness, or resource distribution, individuals retain the explicit right to human review and intervention.

### 2.2 Fairness & Non-Discrimination
* **Algorithmic Parity:** We actively strive to minimize demographic bias in training datasets and production inferences.
* **Continuous Evaluation:** Engineering teams must systematically audit models for algorithmic disparity across protected characteristics, including age, gender, race, and geography.

### 2.3 Transparency & Explainability
* **Right to Explanation:** Users interacting with an AI agent or downstream automated decision must be clearly informed of the AI's involvement.
* **Auditable Explanations:** We pledge to use explainable AI (XAI) frameworks to clarify model logic, ensuring predictions can be traced and understood by non-technical operators.

### 2.4 Privacy & Data Stewardship
* **Data Minimization:** We enforce strict data minimization practices. AI models shall only ingest the bare minimum dataset necessary to execute their core function.
* **Consent Preservation:** Customer data will never be routed to unvetted third-party foundational models for retraining without explicit, trackable opt-in consent.

---

## 3. Structural Governance & Compliance Enforcement
* **The AI Safety Committee:** A multi-disciplinary team consisting of members from Engineering, Legal, and Privacy departments will evaluate every high-risk deployment before release.
* **Voluntary Restraint:** If a model cannot meet the safety, fairness, and interpretability bars established across this toolkit, our organization will voluntarily withhold the product from deployment, prioritizing human safety over speed-to-market.
